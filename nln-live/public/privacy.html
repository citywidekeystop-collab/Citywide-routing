Privacy Notice

Last updated November 21, 2026. This notice applies to NLN's website inquiry experience.

Information we collect

If you send a project inquiry, we collect the name, business name, email, optional phone, selected service and package, domain preference, your message and submission time. Our hosting provider also processes technical request information such as IP addresses for security and operations.

How we use it

We use this information to review and respond to requests, prepare quotes, prevent spam and manage projects. We do not sell inquiry information to advertisers.

Service providers

Our website is hosted on Render. Inquiries may be stored in a Render-hosted database. Website imagery may be loaded from third-party image hosts, which may process ordinary browser requests. Optional email notifications are enabled only after a supported email provider is configured.

Retention and access

Only authorized NLN team members should access the private inquiry inbox. We retain inquiry records as needed for customer service and business operations, subject to applicable law and deletion requests. To request access or deletion, submit a contact request through this website without including sensitive information.

Security

We use HTTPS, controlled administration access and server-side form validation. No web service can promise absolute security. Please do not submit passwords or financial details in inquiries.

Changes

This notice may be updated if our data practices or providers change.

Marketplace customer requests

When you request a professional, NLN collects your name, contact information, requested service category, ZIP code, preferred timing and job description. Requests are held in our private intake system for review. If we find an appropriate professional and you agree to a connection, relevant details may be shared to arrange the service. Submitting alone does not mean a professional was contacted, booked or assigned.

Professional applications

When you apply as a service provider, we collect your business name, contact information, trade categories, cities, ZIP codes served, optional website, applicable license information and application notes. We use this to review your eligibility and potential service-area matches. New applications remain private and pending until a manual verification decision. We do not publish an applicant's personal contact details as a public listing.

Marketplace contact and deletion requests

You may request correction or deletion of submitted Marketplace information by contacting NLN through the website contact page. We may retain records where necessary for legal, security or business recordkeeping obligations. Participation and matching do not guarantee that a job will be accepted or completed.
