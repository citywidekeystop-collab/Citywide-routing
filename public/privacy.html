Privacy Policy

Draft policy — review and finalize for your business before publishing.

When you submit a service request, we collect the information you provide, including your name, phone number, email (if provided), ZIP code, address (if provided), requested service and description of the problem.

How we use your information

We use these details to review your request, contact you about possible referral options, and share the inquiry with a participating locksmith if appropriate. The assigned locksmith handles availability, price and actual service. We may retain records for customer support, compliance, security and business operations.

Who receives request information

Find Me a Locksmith is a referral platform and does not perform locksmith work. A participating independent locksmith provider may receive your request details so they can respond to you. We may also use hosting, database, communication and spam-prevention services to process this information. We do not promise comparison quotes from multiple providers.

Website photos

Service photos are customer-supplied illustrations of service categories, not a representation that pictured people are providers participating in Find Me a Locksmith. Images are hosted on an image CDN.

Text messages and marketing

Contact information is used to respond to the specific inquiry. Optional marketing messages are only sent if you separately opt in, and you may opt out of promotional messages. Message and data rates may apply.

Contact and requests

Contact the Find Me a Locksmith team using the contact details listed on the website to request access to or correction or deletion of your information, subject to legal recordkeeping requirements.

Before launch, the business operator must add a verified business contact, effective date, applicable vendor list, retention schedule and any legally required disclosures.
