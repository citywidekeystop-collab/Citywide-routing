COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you request help.

Will I get quotes from several locksmiths?

Not necessarily. Find Me a Locksmith is a referral platform, not a locksmith business or a guaranteed multi-quote comparison service. We may refer you to a participating provider who handles the work.

Is there a charge to submit a request?

Submitting a service request through this website is free. A professional will discuss service fees, availability and pricing before any work is authorized.

Can I request a commercial locksmith?

Yes. You can request commercial lockouts, lock changes, panic bar hardware and electronic locks. We will confirm which services are available for your job.

Can you guarantee an emergency arrival time?

No. Submit the details so the team can check availability. Do not rely on this website for a life-threatening emergency. Call 911 if you are in immediate danger.
