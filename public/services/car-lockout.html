Locked keys in your car?

Car lockouts happen in parking lots, driveways and at work. Submit the vehicle details and location so dispatch can check for an available automotive locksmith. Have proof of ownership or authorization ready.

How to request this locksmith service

Identify the vehicle

Share the make, model and approximate year in the job description.

Describe the situation

Mention whether the key is in the cabin, trunk or ignition, and whether the locks are electronic.

Confirm service details

Ask the assigned locksmith about the service call charge and quoted total before work begins.

Common reasons customers request help

  • Keys locked inside a car
  • Car door will not unlock
  • Keys stuck in a vehicle trunk

Frequently asked questions

Will a lockout service make a replacement car key?

A car lockout opens a vehicle, while lost-key replacement may involve cutting or programming. Select car key replacement if you no longer have the key.

Do I need identification?

Expect a locksmith to verify ownership or authorization before providing entry.
