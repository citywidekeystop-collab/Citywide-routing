MARYLAND LOCKSMITH REQUESTS

Need a replacement car key?

When a vehicle key is lost or damaged, replacement may require cutting a new blade, programming a transponder or pairing a remote. Submit the vehicle details so dispatch can check the correct service capability.

Request a locksmith →See all locksmith services

How to request this locksmith service

Provide vehicle details

Share vehicle make, model, year and whether you have any working key.

Describe the key

Mention traditional metal key, transponder, push-to-start fob or damaged remote.

Verify ownership

A locksmith may request proof of ownership and identification before cutting or programming a key.

Common reasons customers request help

  • Lost all car keys
  • Replacement transponder key
  • Key fob and remote programming

Frequently asked questions

Can every car key be programmed on site?

Not always. Compatibility, equipment access and key availability vary by vehicle; dispatch can check your request.

What if the key is locked inside instead of lost?

Choose the car lockout service, which is different from making a new key.
