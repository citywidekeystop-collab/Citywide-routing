Need a commercial locksmith?

Businesses may need fast access restoration, controlled key distribution or lock hardware upgrades. Describe the property and required work so dispatch can determine whether Citywide or a participating qualified locksmith can help.

How to request this locksmith service

Identify business access needs

Mention office, storefront, warehouse or other commercial entry and the affected door or lock.

Describe the hardware

State whether you need a mortise cylinder, panic bar, electric strike, keypad or lockout.

Confirm building requirements

Check fire-egress, accessibility and property-manager approvals before authorizing any hardware modification.

Common reasons customers request help

  • Business and office lockout
  • Commercial lock rekey or cylinder change
  • Panic bar, magnetic lock or access control service

Frequently asked questions

Commercial hardware requests can be submitted. Scope and qualified provider availability are confirmed during follow-up.

This site accepts locksmith-related lock and door security hardware jobs, not general carpentry or door repair services.
