How to request this locksmith service

Explain the job

Select your type of lock and describe what is happening. A stuck deadbolt, broken key, or lockout may require different tools.

Share your location

Provide your ZIP code and preferred timing. Service availability depends on the specific job and area.

Discuss your options

A service representative can contact you to discuss timing, the provider and potential charges before you approve any work.

Common reasons customers request help

  • Locked out of your home, vehicle or business
  • Need locks rekeyed after moving or losing a key
  • Want a new deadbolt or smart keypad lock installed

Frequently asked questions

Can I request the closest locksmith?

You can send a local request. Dispatch will review coverage and availability, but the website does not guarantee the closest provider or a specific arrival time.

Will I receive quotes from several locksmiths?

Not necessarily. This is a locksmith request service rather than an independent directory or guaranteed multi-quote comparison.
