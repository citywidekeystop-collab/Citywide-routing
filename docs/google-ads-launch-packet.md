# Find Me a Locksmith — Google Search advertising / Advanced Verification launch packet
Last updated: 2026-11-10

## Identity (do not conflate these businesses)

- Advertising brand: **Find Me a Locksmith**.
- Business model: **independent locksmith referral / lead-generation network**. Customers submit service requests; the platform refers them to independent providers who may accept and fulfill jobs. The platform does not itself perform locksmith work.
- Avoid claiming the platform is a licensed locksmith company or “Google-certified” or that every provider is vetted before license records and provider agreements support those claims.
- **Citywide Roadside Lock & Key** is a separate business. Do not use its name, Maryland license number, Google Ads account, or credentials as though they belong to Find Me a Locksmith.
- Advertising site: https://find-me-a-locksmith.onrender.com (temporary Render domain). Confirm the eventual independent brand domain.

Add real contact phone and email to the website and final policies. Check with a Maryland lawyer/regulator about any registration and referral-business obligations.

4. **15% promotion** — request written participation/approval from each provider and determine eligibility and exclusions. Do not display or advertise the 15% claim until genuinely redeemable; the live site's earlier planned offer was removed from public presentation.
5. **Customer submission backend** — provision a dedicated secure database, proper admin secret, notification delivery, data retention and privacy controls; test end-to-end before turning the intake on. Site currently blocks submissions and indexing on purpose.
6. **Domain and compliance** — configure a domain you control, verify HTTPS and privacy/terms, and configure Google Search Console and analytics when the site is actually ready.
7. **Google Ads account** — create a new Google Ads account for *Find Me a Locksmith*, not Citywide's existing connected account 688-013-2960. Select appropriate business and payments profile. Set campaign PAUSED; no ad spend until compliance and approval.
8. **Advanced Verification** — apply with the new Ads account customer ID and the correct aggregator/lead-generator operating model; use only the platform's real information. Google may require business documentation, licensing evidence (where applicable), and a video verification interview.
9. **Conversion tracking** — track genuine accepted leads only, after consent/privacy review; test for duplicates and fake preview conversion events.
